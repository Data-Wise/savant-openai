# Writing evidence within proof teaching

Preserve the source mathematics and teaching scope. This reference does not authorize general
copyediting, alternate proofs, or new citations. If asked only for grammar or referents, use
an available appropriate writing skill or report that this pedagogy skill is not the owner.

In teaching insertions, inspect each pronoun, possessive, and demonstrative noun phrase in
context and against the actual equation/definition/figure target. Retain clear references and
nonreferential grammatical uses; query competing or missing antecedents rather than guess.
An explicitly exhaustive referent request gets a RETAIN/REVISE/QUERY ledger; do not force that
output on normal proof teaching. Recheck the inserted explanations.

Distinguish defined local labels from claims about scientific properties. A property needs
its object, assumptions, and criterion; a terminology recommendation based on literature
needs primary-text locators, access depth, and a match in meaning. Do not invent sources or
bring in new results to rescue a proof. Query unsupported meaning; retain mathematical-error
and missing-prerequisite escalation. Missing full text is an evidence gap, not proof that a
term is invented or grounds for deleting an existing citation. A voice whitelist proves no
scientific property. Use only selected author/register hyphen preferences and venue rules;
preserve conventional technical terms and mathematical content.
