# Statistics mode

Check:

- estimand, data-generating process, and assumptions;
- identification and estimability;
- estimator definition and numerical stability;
- diagnostics and sensitivity to unverifiable assumptions;
- an independent calculation or reference implementation;
- whether reported precision exceeds Monte Carlo or sampling precision.

Never infer a causal or statistical conclusion from a p-value, fit statistic,
or model output without its assumptions and diagnostics.
